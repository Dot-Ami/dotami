The skills and knowledge lists for each job in data.ts come from the Occupational and Skills
Information System (OaSIS) - 2025 Version 1.0, published by Employment and Social Development Canada:
https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/10ce43bd-fb58-4969-806b-4bffebc87bec
(files skills_oasis_2025_v1.1.csv, knowledge_oasis_2025_v.1.1.csv, labels_oasis_2025_v1.1.csv and
guide_oasis_2025_v4.0.csv; record modified 2026-10-04).

DotAmi keeps each job's name and its ratings, and shows a job's list by name only. The government of
Canada does not endorse DotAmi or anyone's use of this information.

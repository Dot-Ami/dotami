The skills and knowledge lists for each job in data.ts, and the example job titles in titles.ts, come from
the Occupational and Skills Information System (OaSIS) - 2025 Version 1.0, published by Employment and
Social Development Canada:
https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/10ce43bd-fb58-4969-806b-4bffebc87bec
(files skills_oasis_2025_v1.1.csv, knowledge_oasis_2025_v.1.1.csv, labels_oasis_2025_v1.1.csv and
guide_oasis_2025_v4.0.csv, record modified 2026-10-04; and example-titles_oasis_2025_v1.0.csv,
downloaded 2026-10-21).

DotAmi keeps each job's name, its ratings and its example titles, shows a job's list by name only, and
searches the titles for the job a person types. The government of Canada does not endorse DotAmi or
anyone's use of this information.

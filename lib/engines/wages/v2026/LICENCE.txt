The wages in data.ts come from the Wages dataset published by Employment and Social Development Canada
(Job Bank's wage file for 2025):
https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/adad580f-76b0-4502-bd05-20c125de9116
(file 2a71-das-wage2025opendata-esdc-all-19nov2025-vf.csv; revised 2025-11-19).

DotAmi keeps each row's job group, geography, source code, method, basis, low, median and high, reference
period, comment and revision date, as the file gives them. They are what workers in a job group were paid,
never anyone's income. The government of Canada does not endorse DotAmi or anyone's use of this information.

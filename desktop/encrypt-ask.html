Lock your data file with a key?

DotAmi can now lock its data file — your ideas, figures, expense records, statements and settings — and its safety copies with a key that only your Windows account on this computer can open.

What it protects: an administrator account while you're signed out, a copy of the data folder (on a USB stick, in a synced folder, in another computer's backup), or this computer's disk read outside Windows can't read it. It is only as strong as your Windows password.

What it doesn't: anything running as you can still open it, as DotAmi does. Copies Windows or a synced folder already made aren't changed, and on a solid-state disk the old file's bytes may stay on the disk; disk encryption covers those.

If the key is ever lost — your Windows profile is reset, an administrator resets your password, or the data folder is moved to another account or computer — everything not in a backup is lost. An older DotAmi can't open the file afterwards; going back to one needs a backup.

Never lock the data file?

Your data file stays unencrypted: anyone who can read your data folder, a copy of it, or this computer's disk outside Windows can read your ideas, figures, expense records and statements. Disk encryption still protects a lost or stolen computer.

DotAmi won't ask again. You can turn encryption on later in Settings → Data and backups.
